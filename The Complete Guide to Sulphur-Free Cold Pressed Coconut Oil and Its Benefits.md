# The Complete Guide to Sulphur-Free Cold Pressed Coconut Oil and Its Benefits

## Executive Summary

Sekkadi Cold Pressed Coconut Oil represents the gold standard in traditional oil-making. It is carefully produced from **sulphur-free copra** (the dried meat of the coconut fruit) using a **traditional wooden crusher**.

After extraction, the oil undergoes **natural sedimentation under sunlight for 3–5 days** and is **cloth-filtered** to ensure maximum purity without chemical processing.

Coconut oil is unique as it naturally contains **Lauric Acid**, a component also found in **mother’s milk**. It is rich in **Vitamin E** and essential minerals such as **iron** and **zinc**.

**Pure Cold Pressed Coconut Oil** enhances food with its authentic aroma and flavor while preserving essential nutrients.

Coconut oil is widely valued for treating various skin conditions.
