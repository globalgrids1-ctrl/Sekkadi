## 🌿 Rediscover Traditional Goodness

In a time when refined and processed oils dominate the market, **Pure Cold Pressed Groundnut Oil** brings you back to authentic, time-tested methods of oil extraction. Often referred to as the *“Queen of oil,”* this traditional oil is valued for its purity, nutrition, and natural flavor.

Sekkadi cold pressed groundnut oil is extracted using traditional wooden presses known as **Vaagai Marachekku**. The oil is made from high-quality country groundnut seeds sourced directly from local village farmers. This ensures freshness, authenticity, and superior quality.

Unlike refined oils, this product is:
- ✅ Free from chemicals

- ✅ Unrefined and unbleached
