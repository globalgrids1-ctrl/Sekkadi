## 🏺 Made as Ancestors Made It

Creating ghee the traditional way demands patience.

Fresh butter churned from cow's milk is heated slowly over controlled flames in wide vessels:

1. Water evaporates  
2. Milk solids separate  
3. Butter clarifies into liquid gold  

The process cannot be rushed. Skilled artisans carefully monitor temperature to preserve delicate nutrients and achieve perfect clarification.

Afterward, the ghee is filtered through fine cloth, emerging pure and golden.

**No chemicals.  
No preservatives.  
No additives.**

Just pure cow ghee with complete nutritional integrity.

## 🧬 The Remarkable Compound: Butyrate

Butyrate, a short-chain fatty acid found in ghee, plays a crucial role in digestive health:

- Fuels colon cells  
- Supports intestinal barrier integrity  
- Helps reduce inflammation  
- Nourishes beneficial gut bacteria  
- Supports microbiome balance  

Traditional diets rich in ghee provided this compound naturally. Regular consumption delivers butyrate in a form your body easily recognizes and utilizes.
