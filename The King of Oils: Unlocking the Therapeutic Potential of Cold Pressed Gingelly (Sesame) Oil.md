# The King of Oils: Unlocking the Therapeutic Potential of Cold Pressed Gingelly (Sesame) Oil

## Executive Summary

In the realm of traditional wellness, **Pure Cold Pressed Gingelly (Sesame) Oil** holds a distinguished position, often revered as the **"King of Oils."**

# Therapeutic Benefits and Clinical Insights

## 1. Anti-Inflammatory Properties

Inflammation is often a precursor to chronic diseases caused by free radicals. **Sekkadi Cold-Pressed Sesame Oil** acts as a potent defense mechanism.

- **Rich in Zinc and Copper**  
  Supports the production of red blood cells, improves blood circulation, and boosts metabolism.

- **Arthritis Relief**

## 2. Cardiovascular Protection

This oil is a powerhouse for heart health, containing approximately **82% unsaturated fatty acids**.

### Key Benefits

- **Heart Health**  
  Contains essential **Omega-6 fatty acids** crucial for cardiovascular function.

- **Cholesterol Management**  
  Helps lower **LDL cholesterol and triglycerides**, reducing the risk of heart attacks and strokes.

## 3. Diabetes Management

Sesame oil has shown promising results in regulating blood sugar levels in individuals with **Type 2 diabetes**.

- **Glucose Control**  
  Studies suggest that incorporating sesame oil into a diet over a **90-day period** may help reduce both **fasting blood sugar and HbA1c levels**.

The presence of **magnesium** and a powerful anti-cancer compound known as **phytate** makes sesame oil a beneficial dietary addition.
