## 🌿 Executive Summary

Within the sphere of traditional wellness, **Pure Cold Pressed Gingelly (Sesame) Oil** occupies a place of honor, frequently called the **"King of Oils."**

**Sekkadi Cold Pressed Gingelly/Sesame Oil** is sourced directly from local villagers and extracted from premium country black and brown sesame seeds.

To guarantee the highest purity, the oil is produced alongside **Karupatti (natural palm jaggery)** using a traditional wooden crusher known as **Vaagai Marachekku**.

This slow, low-heat extraction process preserves essential nutrients including calcium, iron, phosphorus, copper, and Vitamins D, E, and K.
