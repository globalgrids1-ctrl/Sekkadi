Pure Mountain Honey is a rare and authentic natural sweetener sourced from the pristine forests of the Western Ghats. Sekkadi Mountain Honey is traditionally gathered by indigenous tribal communities from honeycombs found on rocky cliffs. This honey is produced by forest bees that collect nectar from a wide variety of wildflowers, giving it a rich nutritional profile and a distinctive amber-copper color that reflects its purity and natural origin.
Traditional Harvesting and Natural Purity
The harvesting of Sekkadi Mountain Honey follows time-honored tribal practices that respect nature and preserve the ecosystem. The honey is collected in its raw form, retaining natural elements such as beeswax and pollen. This traditional process ensures that Pure Mountain Honey remains free from artificial processing, additives, or preservatives, delivering honey in its most natural and wholesome state.
